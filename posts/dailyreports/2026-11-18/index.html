主要方向：

  • 基因编辑的安全性与精准度提升
  • 神经退行性疾病（如阿尔茨海默症）与慢性疼痛的发病机制及性别差异研究

技术亮点：

  • Superb-seq：集成CRISPR与单细胞RNA测序，实现对基因编辑脱靶效应的全面监测。
  • TrAP：基于AI的RNA测序工具，优化LINE-1等重复序列的转录定量，提高分析精度。

To understand genes and pathways that drive CRC under metabolic stress, we performed Sleeping Beauty (SB) transposon mutagenesis in the intestine of mice fed a high-fat diet (HFD). Cancer immunotherapy is used to treat several cancers by regulating both innate, CD47 and adaptive, PD-L1 immunity. ROS generation can modulate the immune system and tumor cell death. This study investigates the potential of maltol for CRC immunotherapy using CRC patient-derived organoids (PDOs), CRC cell lines, and a mouse xenograft tumor model.
