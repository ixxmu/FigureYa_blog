We evaluated the effect of formalin fixation duration on RNA integrity and on downstream analysis using the GeoMx Digital Spatial Profiler (DSP) whole transcriptome assay and the RNAscope POLR2A assay. Macroscopically normal kidney cortical tissue from three patients was fixed in neutral buffered formalin for 1, 2, 7, or 14 days prior to paraffin embedding. Principal component analysis and pairwise correlation (Pearson r = 0.95) showed that fixation duration had a minimal effect on global gene expression in the GeoMx DSP data, with only a small number of genes differentially expressed. RNAscope POLR2A spot counts per cell were likewise unaffected by fixation duration and were consistent with GeoMx DSP POLR2A counts. These findings indicate that both GeoMx DSP and RNAscope are tolerant of moderate RNA degradation. Incorporation of DV200 measurement as a pre-analytical quality-control metric, combined with RNAscope validation, provides a time- and cost-effective framework for FFPE-based GeoMx DSP spatial transcriptomic studies.
