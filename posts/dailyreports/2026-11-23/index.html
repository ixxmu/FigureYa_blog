To elucidate these dynamics, we integrated multimodal molecular profiling, comprising whole-exome sequencing, RNA-sequencing, cyclic immunofluorescence, and spatial transcriptomics, across a baseline ITM discovery cohort, multiple independent validation cohorts, and dense longitudinal tracking of four patients with divergent clinical trajectories. Longitudinal evolutionary tracking in patients with paired primary, ITMs and for some distant metastasis, revealed that non-progressing ITMs maintain a primary-like differentiated state, whereas progressing tumors undergo profound dedifferentiation. AD knock-in mice expressing signaling-deficient p75NTR variants in astrocytes showed enhanced Aβ burden, brain histopathology and cognitive impairment, even when variants were introduced late in the disease process.
